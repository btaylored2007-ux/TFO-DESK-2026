# Coverage matrix v1.2.0

Representative coverage, not exhaustive. GICS structure per MSCI methodology (E17): 11 sectors.

## GICS sectors and metric interpretation (expanded in v1.2.0)

| GICS sector | Module / lessons | Key metrics taught | Limitations noted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | M13 l1, l5, l6; M6 | crack spread, reserve life, breakeven, cost/boe, operating leverage to price | spot-price valuation trap; reserve life is a ratio, not a production promise |
| Materials | M13 l2, l6 | grade, AISC/C1, JORC reserves vs resources, mine life | company cost definitions vary; reserve codes differ |
| Industrials | M15 l1, l6 | backlog coverage, book-to-bill, RASK-CASK, load factor, fixed-price overrun | fuel and FX can erase thin airline unit margins |
| Consumer Discretionary | M15 l2, l6 | same-store sales (traffic x ticket), inventory turns | one-quarter trends; credit sensitivity |
| Consumer Staples | M15 l2, l6 | volume vs price/mix, gross margin | price-led growth may fade |
| Health Care | M15 l3, l6 | pipeline phase, patent cliff, reimbursement, TGA/FDA/EMA roles | binary events; no unsourced success-rate percentages |
| Financials | M12 (all); M11 | NIM, cost-to-income, loan loss rate, CET1 (general term), combined ratio, float, AUM fees, P/B, ROE | national capital numbers not newly verified as legal minima |
| Information Technology | M14 l1, l2, l6 | ARR, NRR, rule of 40, SBC, FCF after SBC, book-to-bill, inventory days | company-defined metrics; dilution |
| Communication Services | M14 l3, l6 | ARPU, churn, capex intensity, users, ad revenue/user | user growth vs monetisation; spectrum capex |
| Utilities | M13 l3, l6 | RAB, allowed return, gearing | allowed return is not a guaranteed shareholder return |
| Real Estate | M15 l4, l6 | cap rate, NTA, FFO, gearing, WALE, occupancy | valuations lag; NTA is not a hard floor |
