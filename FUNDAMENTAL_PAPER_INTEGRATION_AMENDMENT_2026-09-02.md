# Fundamental research-to-PAPER integration amendment — 2026-09-02

PAPER ONLY. No broker, credential, live order or live-capital path is authorised.
This additive amendment was sealed before any new-feature-attributed PAPER entry.
It does not edit or reinterpret the frozen research-successor registration.

## Funder direction and scope

On 2026-09-02 the funder directed that every deployed signal-producing research
feature be capable of reaching the existing autonomous fundamental PAPER book so
the desk can measure forward results. This is an integration of evidence into
the already registered fundamental decision process, not permission to promote
an intermediate feature label into a trading verdict.

`filing_diff`, `event_queue`, and the six sector adapters may originate or enrich
a research case. They become capital-eligible only when a separately completed,
hash-verified block independently satisfies every existing strict decision and
execution condition: exact `undervalued`, exact `LONG`, positive future
catalyst, future thesis-review date, substantive `KILLS IT`, stable issuer ID,
verified exact execution route, and an external deterministic allocator receipt.
`RESEARCH_READY` and `MATERIAL_LEAD` remain evidence states and can never be
translated into `undervalued` or `LONG` by the integration layer.

## Immediate PAPER route

1. A feature may place an issuer into the research/adjudication queue.
2. A strict completed block is hash sealed and reparsed from source.
3. The existing deterministic allocator alone sets USD notional against live
   cash and registered concentration limits.
4. The enrichment stage binds that receipt to the verified research batch.
5. A venue-session gate advances an eligible row only to dry run.
6. The existing PAPER opener repeats market-state, route, currency, FX,
   liquidity, spread, cash, name, sector, currency-bloc and short-gross gates.
7. A passed entry records feature IDs, stable issuer ID and execution-manifest
   hash in both the position and append-only entry receipt.

This route is autonomous within the existing standing schedule: no human
confirmation and no new per-run trade cap are added. A failed gate remains a
refusal and must not be worked around. The integration is long-only; it does not
expand the desk's short-expression authority.

## Measurement

The six adapter families are prospectively reserved as six house trials at the
pre-outcome trial floor of 3,644, producing a new conservative floor of 3,650.
Filing diff and event queue are recorded as provenance, not counted as separate
alpha variants. Standalone adapter capital proposals remain subject to the
original 182-day/100-observation validation bar and sealed US$10,000 probe rule;
that bar is distinct from using adapter evidence inside an independently valid
main-book fundamental decision.

## Non-signal controls

Coverage accounting, source quotas, manifest integrity, fill verification,
fully-loaded NAV, concentration reporting, carry-hurdle monitoring and futures
panel readiness can withhold, quarantine or measure. They cannot originate a
trade or create an undervaluation verdict.

all 133 carry `NEITHER`. The correct immediate allocation is therefore $0. The
first future feature-attributed block that passes every condition above is
eligible for autonomous PAPER entry; no synthetic trade is manufactured merely
to exercise the machinery.
